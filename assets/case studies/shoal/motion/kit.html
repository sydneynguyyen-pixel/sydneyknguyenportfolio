The Shoal prototype beside a 3D model of the kit: four clear jars labelled You, Mom, Bill and Sam, and a round charging puck. A spare stone on the puck glows white. In the app you pair it to a task, and the stone turns yellow because the task is due soon. It goes into your jar. Tapping Find stone makes it blink, and marking the task done turns its light off. The other jars hold the family's stones, glowing blue for tasks someone is doing and yellow for tasks due soon.
